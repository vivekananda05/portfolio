01 · About

Research at the intersection of vision & language.

I am a PhD candidate in the Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering (CSRE) at IIT Bombay, advised by Dr. Biplab Banerjee. My research explores multimodal AI systems that align vision, language, and reasoning.

My current work centers on prompt learning in vision-language models and prompt-based security and privacy for vision tasks, advancing trustworthy visual understanding.

PhD theme

  1. 01

    Adapting vision-language models with learned prompts, both at training time and at test time.

  2. 02

02 · Research

Selected publication.

BMVC 2026 · Accepted

From Patches to Pixels: Dual-Branch Prompt Learning for Hyperspectral Scene Generalization

Vivekananda Giri, Ushasi Chaudhuri, Biplab Banerjee

British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC), 2026

A dual-branch patch-to-pixel framework that unifies CLIP patch tokens and frozen SAM-2 pixel tokens via a Token-wise Cross-Scale Distillation Module (TCDM), a Prompt-Conditioned Low-Rank Adapter (PCLRA) with hierarchical orthogonality, and a Gated Product-of-Experts (GPoE) fusion, achieving scene-agnostic generalization with entirely frozen backbones.
